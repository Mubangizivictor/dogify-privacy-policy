Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 20, 2024

Welcome to Dogify. Your privacy is critically important to us. This policy explains how we handle your data when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a personalized dog training experience, we collect the following:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and profile picture (via Firebase Auth).
  • Dog Profile: Information you provide about your dog's breed, age, and training goals.
  • Usage Data: Training progress, completed sessions, and interactions with our AI Assistant.

2. Third-Party Services

We use industry-standard providers to ensure your app works smoothly:

  • Google Play Services: For app distribution and core functionality.
  • Firebase (Google): We use Firebase for secure login, hosting our database (Firestore), and tracking app crashes (Crashlytics).
  • AI Assistant: Your text queries are processed to provide training advice. We do not sell this data to third parties.

3. How We Use Data

We use your information to:

  • Personalize your dog's training curriculum.
  • Keep your training progress synced across devices.
  • Improve our AI Assistant's accuracy and helpfulness.
  • Send essential service updates via email.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your data as long as your account is active. You have full control:

You can delete your account and all associated personal data at any time directly through the Profile Settings menu within the Dogify app. Once deleted, this action cannot be undone.

5. Security

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Dogify does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 13. If you believe we have collected such data, please contact us immediately.
