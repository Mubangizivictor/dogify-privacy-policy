1. Introduction

Welcome to Dogify. We understand that your privacy and your dog's data are important. This policy describes our practices regarding information collected through our mobile application.

By using Dogify, you consent to the practices described in this policy. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

2. Data Collection

To provide high-quality training sessions and AI assistance, we collect:

A. Personal Information

  • Account Details: Email, name, and profile picture provided via Firebase Authentication.
  • Dog Profiles: Name, breed, age, and training goals.

B. Usage & Technical Data

  • Training Progress: Sessions completed, streaks, and curriculum milestones.
  • Local Storage: We use Hive to cache training data on your device for offline access.
  • AI Interactions: Text queries sent to our AI Assistant to generate training advice.
  • Crash Reports: We use Firebase Crashlytics to monitor app stability.

3. How We Use Data

Dogify uses your information to deliver a personalized experience:

  • Personalization: Adapting the training curriculum to your dog's specific breed and behavior.
  • Synchronization: Using our SyncService to keep your data updated across all your devices.
  • Notifications: Sending training reminders and tips (you can opt-out in settings).
  • AI Training: Improving the responses of our AI Trainer based on common queries.

4. Third-Party Services

We use trusted partners to ensure the app functions securely:

  • Firebase (Google): For authentication, cloud database storage, and app analytics.
  • Google Fonts: To provide high-quality typography within the interface.
  • Notification Services: To deliver alerts to your device.

5. Data Deletion & Control

We believe you should have total control over your data. In compliance with Google Play requirements:

How to delete your account:

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data (including dog profiles and training history) directly inside the app by going to:
Profile > Settings > Delete My Account

Alternatively, you can request manual deletion by emailing us at mubangizivic@gmail.com. We will process your request and purge all data from our Firebase servers within 48 hours.
